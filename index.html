about the brand

In the early 80s, our family began crafting high-performance cricket bats for the world's top brands. What started as a behind-the-scenes journey taught us the art of performance — from top-tier players and their exacting needs.

Today, as a family of master bat makers, backed by skilled artisans, we create world-class cricket equipment under our own name — DSC.

our craftmanship

Every great bat starts with a great cleft. We source only the finest English Willow and Kashmir Willow, and each piece is seasoned and shaped by hand. Our artisans blend traditional techniques with modern precision to deliver unmatched performance and durability.The same detail and craftsmanship flow into our gloves, pads, helmets, and shoes — ensuring every cricketer experiences the DSC standard.
