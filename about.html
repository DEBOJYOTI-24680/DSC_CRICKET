about the brand

From Punjab, India since 1980s

In the early 80s, our family began crafting high-performance cricket bats for the world's top brands. What started as a behind-the-scenes journey taught us the art of performance — from top-tier players and their exacting needs. Today, as a family of master bat makers, backed by skilled artisans, we create world-class cricket equipment under our own name — DSC.

Every bat begins with premium clefts of English or Kashmir Willow and is handcrafted for balance, power, and precision. From gloves and pads to helmets and shoes, our commitment to fearless design runs across the entire range.
